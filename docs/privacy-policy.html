Privacy Policy

RATED — Universal Tennis Ranking  |  Effective date: 11 May 2026

This policy explains what personal data RATED collects when you use our app, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and what rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who We Are

RATED (“we”, “our”, “the app”) is a tennis ranking platform for club players. The data controller responsible for your personal data is:

  • Contact email: bikasantonis@gmail.com

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, please reach out at the address above.

2. Data We Collect

2a. When you sign in with Google

When you choose “Sign in with Google”, Google shares the following with RATED via OAuth 2.0 (scopes: email, profile):

DataHow it is used
Email addressIdentifies your account; used for notifications
Display namePre-fills your RATED profile name (editable)
Profile photo URLPre-fills your avatar (replaceable at any time)

We do not request access to your Google Calendar, Contacts, Gmail, or any other Google service.

2b. When you register with email

We collect your display name, email address, and a hashed password. We record the timestamp of your explicit consent to this Privacy Policy (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).

2c. Match and ranking data

We store your match history, ELO rating, ELO tier, win/loss record, and peak ELO. This is the core purpose of the app — to maintain a fair and verifiable ranking.

2d. Location data (optional, opt-in)

If you enable the Location feature in Settings, we collect your approximate home location (rounded to ~1 km precision) and store it as a city name plus rounded coordinates. This is used to:

  • Show tournaments near you
  • Show players near you on the leaderboard
  • Send push notifications about tournaments opening in your area

Location data is collected only after your explicit consent. You can withdraw consent and delete your location data at any time from Settings → Location.

2e. Device and usage data

Our crash-reporting tool (Sentry) automatically collects device type, OS version, and crash stack traces when the app encounters an error. This data does not identify you personally and is used solely to fix bugs.

5. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data is retained for as long as your account exists.
  • Deleted accounts: When you delete your account, all personally identifiable information is permanently erased within 30 days. Match records are anonymised (player references are removed) so historical ranking statistics remain accurate.
  • Location data: Deleted immediately when you revoke location consent.

6. Your Rights (GDPR Arts. 15–22)

As a data subject you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data (you can edit your profile directly in the app).
  • Erasure — delete your account and all associated PII via Settings → Delete account.
  • Portability — request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Restriction — ask us to stop processing your data in certain circumstances.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — for consent-based processing (location, notifications) at any time via Settings.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at bikasantonis@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority (in Greece: www.dpa.gr).

7. Security

Your data is stored in Supabase's EU infrastructure with row-level security policies ensuring users can only access their own records. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Location coordinates are rounded before storage (data minimisation, GDPR Art. 5(1)(c)).

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be communicated via an in-app notice. The effective date at the top of this page always reflects the latest version.
